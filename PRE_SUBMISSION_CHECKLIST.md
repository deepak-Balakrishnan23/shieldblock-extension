# Pre-Submission Checklist

- Verify the privacy policy URL is live and matches the current code.
- Verify the Web Store listing does not promise unsupported claims.
- Smoke-test on YouTube, news sites, shopping sites, and social feeds.
- Confirm toggles, allowlist, and Teach This Site work after reload.
- Confirm no remote code or third-party analytics were introduced.
- Verify permission descriptions in the listing are accurate.
- Verify the extension still works with cookies, phishing, and AI layers disabled individually.
- Review false positives on product cards, navigation menus, and article sidebars.
- Rebuild imported rules if filter sources were refreshed.
- Capture screenshots of Stats, AI, Filters, Allowlist, and Site Insight.
- Rebuild the zip before upload.
